# Learning: the exam (brief, exam window, result, My attempts, My certificates)

The brief, exam window, result and Review Answers are drawn from the owner's four designs of 30 Oct 2026 (`docs/designs/exam-brief-v2.png`, `exam-question-v2.png`, `exam-result-v2.png`, `exam-review-answers.png`) under one rule: **a single view, most of the data visible without scrolling**; the other screens have no PNG. The reference for the behaviour is the website's Learning Center (`PaperBrief`, `PaperUnlock`, `ExamWindow`, `PaperExam`, `SubmitOverviewDialog`, `ResultSummary`, `AttemptSummary`, `MyAttempts`, `Certificate`, `CertificatePage`, `MyCertificatesPage`, `CertificateVerify`) and the API (`learning.service.ts` `getPaperForCandidate` / `submitAttempt` / `getMyAttempt`, `SubmitAttemptDto`, `paper-checkout.service.ts`, `learning-plan-access.service.ts`), done as a phone does it. Code: `lib/features/learning_exam/`. Tests: `test/learning_exam_test.dart` (logic, 71), `test/learning_exam_screen_test.dart` (screens, 129), `test/golden/learning_exam_golden_test.dart` (16 goldens, at 390 wide and the design's own height: brief 912, question 752, result 742, review 927), `test/live/learning_exam_live_test.dart` (16, against the local API); fakes and recorded API answers in `test/learning_exam_fakes.dart` and `test/fixtures/learning_exam/`. Data: `tool/seed/learning-flows.ts`.

## How it is entered

* `Future<void> openPaper(BuildContext context, PaperRef paper, {ExamRepository? repository})` (`open_paper.dart`; `PaperRef` and the call are as the stub had them, `repository` is a seam for tests). It completes when the whole flow is closed — the brief, the exam, its result, **and any retake started from the result** (since 30 Oct 2026: a retake used to be a flow of its own, so the programme page behind read its attempts after the first result and still said "Not passed yet" after the retake passed).. It builds its own repository (`AppConfig.demo ? DemoExamRepository() : ApiExamRepository(api: context.read<ApiClient>())`), opens the **brief** as a page (`showBriefPage`, a `Navigator.push` that hands back the sitting to start), then the **exam** as a plain `Navigator.push` route (no tab bar, no shell), then the **result** in its place; the result's Review answers pushes **Review Answers** over it. The future completes when the member has left the flow (closed the brief, left the exam, closed the result), so a landing can refresh its best scores and attempt counts after it.
* `MyAttemptsScreen({required VoidCallback onBack})` and `CertificatesScreen({required VoidCallback onBack})` are the hub's two doors; each builds its own controller. They draw their own title bar and expect the hub to push them under `ShellPage(tab: AppTab.learning)`. A result or certificate they open is a full-screen route over that.
* Signed-in person: `Provider<AuthController>` when there is one (its id keys a saved sitting, its first name greets the result). Without it (a bare test tree) the id is `me`.

## The flow, with the endpoints

1. **Brief** (`brief_screen.dart`, `BriefController`). `GET /learning/papers/:slug` is the paper with its questions (the answer key stripped) and is also the access check: it answers 403 `PAPER_LOCKED` when the member has not opened it. Alongside: `GET /learning/me/attempts?paperId=` (attempts used, best score) and `GET /learning/me/certificates` (a certificate held for this paper); the saved sitting from the device store. If the paper is locked: its parameters come from the public `GET /learning/papers?kind=` (found by slug; the other kind is tried if the landing had it wrong), what it costs from `GET /learning/papers/:slug/quote`, and the way in from the 403's body (`reason`, `opens`, `remaining`, `plan`).
2. **Opening a locked paper**, three ways, exactly the website's `PaperUnlockPanel`: `POST /learning/me/opens {target, targetId}` ("Open with my plan", only when `reason` is `PICK`: the plan has a place free for the whole course or mock exam), `POST /learning/papers/:slug/redeem` (ICR credits, for good, with a confirmation naming what will be left), or the paper's page on the website (payment is PayU, which the app does not do). After either write the brief loads again and is ready.
3. **Exam** (`exam_screen.dart`, `ExamController`). One question at a time under the clock; answers, flags and the place in the paper live in the controller and are written to the device after every change.
4. **Submit** = `POST /learning/papers/:slug/attempts {startedAt: epoch ms, answers: [{questionId, selected: [int]}]}`, every served question in the list (an empty list would be scored against the whole bank), the picks sorted. The answer is `{attempt, certificate, review}`.
5. **Result** (`result_screen.dart`) and **Review Answers** (`review_answers_screen.dart`, the question-by-question review that used to sit on the result). Reopened later from the brief's My attempts, or from My attempts, with `GET /learning/me/attempts/:id` (the same shape; `attempt_result_loader.dart`).
6. **My certificates** reads `GET /learning/me/certificates`; the register is `GET /learning/certificates/:number` (public).

### Correctness of the exam (the part that must not fail)

* **The clock is the wall clock.** The time left is always `startedAt + time limit - now`, recomputed on every tick and when the app returns to the front (`WidgetsBindingObserver`), so a throttled or stopped timer in the background can neither grant nor lose time. The server does not enforce time (the DTO only takes `startedAt`); the app does. Proved: logic `time spent in the background is still spent...`, screen `the app going to the background and coming back recomputes the clock`, `the app coming back after the time ran out sends the answers`.
* **Answers survive.** They are in the controller, not the widgets, so a rebuild or a rotation cannot lose one (`a rotation keeps the question, the answers and the clock`); and every change is written to the keystore (`flutter_secure_storage`, a copy in memory if it fails) as a snapshot of the sitting, the questions exactly as served (a randomised paper resumes in the order the candidate saw), when the clock started, where they were, what they answered and flagged. A killed app finds it again on the brief ("Resume - 18:00 left") or, if the time ran out meanwhile, sends what was answered (`a whole new screen on a saved sitting...`, `a screen opened on a sitting whose time ran out sends its answers at once`, logic `a sitting picked up after its time ran out is sent at once`, `the answers are kept to the store as they change...`, `a failing store does not stop the exam`).
* **One submit, one attempt.** Two `submit()` calls share one send (logic `pressing Submit twice sends one sitting`; live `pressing Submit twice makes one attempt`). Nothing is editable once a send has started (logic `nothing is writable once the answers are on their way`).
* **A failed send keeps everything and can be retried**, and a retry first asks the API whether the sitting already landed (it looks for an attempt with the same `startedAt` and reads it back through `GET /me/attempts/:id`), so a reply that never arrived does not become a second attempt. Proved in logic (`a retry first looks for the sitting that may have landed...`) and live (`a reply that never arrived is not sent twice...`: the real API recorded the sitting, the app was told it failed, the retry created no second attempt).

## What the API cannot do, and what the screens do instead

* **It never enforces the time limit** (the DTO takes only `startedAt`), and holds no sitting in progress. The clock and the saved sitting are the app's (above). A sitting sent after its time (the app was away) is sent as it is; the server records a `timeTakenMs` longer than the limit.
* **It cannot delete an attempt** or give back credits. The live tests that sit papers leave their attempts (see the test's header) and the one that redeems does it as the throwaway Exam Persona.
* **A locked paper's parameters are not on the 403.** The brief reads them from the public list (`GET /learning/papers`, about 100 KB, only for a locked paper), and the price from the quote.
* **There is no per-topic breakdown.** The result derives one from the `category` of each reviewed question (and says nothing when there is no category).
* **The review is rebuilt from the live question bank** (`getMyAttempt`): a question edited since changes what an old sitting appears to have been marked against; one deleted is missing, and the result says so. A later pass returns the certificate already held in its own result (the website says the same), but a reopened later sitting does not claim it (live test `redeem, fail on a blank sitting, pass on the key...` covers both).
* **Payment** is PayU, run in the app since 2 Nov 2026: "Pay now" calls `POST /learning/papers/:slug/checkout` and posts the gateway's form in the app's own payment view (`lib/features/payments/`).
* **The certificate PDF is built and saved in the app** (the owner's call, 2 Nov 2026; it was "Download PDF on the website" before, which opened `/learning/certificates/<paper slug>` and asked the browser to be signed in). `certificate_pdf.dart` is a port of the website's `certificatePdf.ts`: the same hand-written PDF (A4 landscape, 842 by 595 pt; Helvetica and Helvetica-Bold, Times-BoldItalic for the name's fallback, nothing embedded, so the text is selectable; the vector QR from `qr_code.dart`, the same code as the website's; the frame, wedges, tinted panel and every position as the TS has them). Text is centred with Helvetica's own advance widths, compiled in as tables, where the website measures with a canvas. `certificate_marks.dart` reads the pictures (ICR's logo, the seal, the partner and the signature from the bundle, the awarding body's logo from the network, the holder's name set by the text engine) and flattens each onto the colour it sits on (`#F2F6FA` on the panel, white for the signature), as the website does, so none prints in a white box; a mark that fails is dropped, never an error. "Download PDF" then hands the bytes to `FilePicker.saveFile` (the system "Save as" sheet, Downloads by default on Android): "Saved ICR-….pdf", nothing when the sheet is closed, "Couldn't make the PDF. Try again." when anything throws. The seams are `CertificatePdfServices` (`fetch`, `renderName`, `marks`, `save`).
* **Question types.** `SINGLE` and `MULTIPLE` exist. The website draws both as radio buttons (a multiple-choice question there can never score); the app draws and sends a multiple-choice answer properly (live `a multiple-choice question is right only when every right option is picked`).

## Deviations from the brief and from the website

* **The link Share hands over is the register's page** (`/learning/verify?id=<number>`), not `/learning/certificates/<id>`: on the website `/learning/certificates/[slug]` is the holder's own copy, found by *paper slug* and behind sign-in, so it cannot take a certificate number; the verify page is the public one (the website's own "Share" uses it too).
* **The holder's name is in Work Sans italic**, not Tangerine (not bundled), and the on-screen certificate is a scaled sheet you pinch, not a re-flowed page.
* **The PDF differs from the website's in four small ways.** The name is rendered by the text engine in Roboto bold italic, gold on white at four times its printed size (the website rasterises Tangerine from its webfont, which the app does not bundle), the same face the sheet shows. The level line is the app's sheet's (`levelLabel`, the assessment ladder's names: "PROFESSIONAL LEVEL" for an INTERMEDIATE paper), where the website's screen and PDF both print "INTERMEDIATE LEVEL" (they read `LEARNING_LEVEL_LABELS`); the app's own sheet already read the assessment name, and the PDF now matches the sheet it was made from. Pictures are stored with Flate (the website uses JPEG) and no wider than 600 px. And a certificate number too long for the last column (`ICR-COMMUNICAT-2026-01AZRIE7` is 182 pt at 11 pt, 164 pt of room) is set smaller instead of running off the page as it would on the website.
* **No "Preparing your assessment" interstitial** (the website holds the candidate for 2.4 s): the clock starts when the exam opens, which is when Take test is tapped.
* **A practice (mock) paper's result no longer has a pass-mark line** ("Below the 70% pass mark"): the design has only "You scored 0%", red below the mark and green at or above it. A certifying paper still says "You passed … against a pass mark of 70%" or "the pass mark is 70%", and fails in red.
* **The brief, exam, result and Review Answers were redrawn from four designs (30 Oct 2026), under "a single view, most of the data visible without scrolling"**, and a few things the designs do not draw are kept or named: Previous is gone from the exam (a number in the strip goes back; the strip shows the two before the open question and the one after it); Clear sits at the right of "Answer"; "N answered" is off the exam bar (the strip's colours say what is answered, the overview names the paper); a flagged question is an amber outline on its number and a filled bookmark on Review, where the palette sheet keeps its own five colours; the Take test button is the unlock action on a locked paper (above); the brief drops the cover, the description, the "Unlocked for you" and "Certificate on a qualifying pass" pills and "Your best so far" (the table and the attempts list say all of it) and always says Take test, never Retake test.
* **Type sizes (owner's rule, 30 Oct 2026: never larger than the design draws).** Each size was found by measuring a line of the design against Roboto, the app's face: the largest of 12 / 13 / 14 / 16 / 18 / 22 whose line is no wider than the design's (`ExamText.at(step, …, line:)` in `exam_styles.dart` gives the step back as the size `AppText.sp` snaps to itself, with the design's own line pitch). So the prompt is 16 on a 26 dp line, the options 14 on 19.5, the question number and the brief's title 18, the bars' and section titles 16, the result's headline 18 (it was 22) and its tiles 18, the review's question 16, the explanation 12, chips and lines 12-14. This replaces the 29 Oct sizes, which were chosen by eye to keep a question in one view; the design's own spacing now does that.
* **The result has no bottom bar any more** (the design has none): "View certificate" is on the certificate notice, Back is the title bar's arrow, "Retake test" is the page's one button, and "Download PDF" is not drawn there (the PDF is made from the certificate sheet, above).
* **"Formative" / "Summative"**: the chip beside the tag is the design's word for a practice paper, and "Summative" for a paper that certifies; the model carries only `certificateEnabled`, not a kind of that name.
* **Marks per question** ("0 / 1 Mark") are not in the API's review. Review Answers uses the review's `marks` if it ever sends one, else the marks of the sitting just taken (from the served questions), else one a question when the attempt's total marks equal its number of questions, and otherwise draws no mark line rather than guess. The API could send `marks` (and `difficulty` on a review) with each reviewed question; nothing else would change.
* **A question's topic** is shown only in the explanation panel ("Core Topic: ..."); a question with no explanation shows neither, as the design hides the panel. The "By topic" card the result used to carry is gone with the rest of what the design leaves off that page; it is not on Review Answers either.
* **Leaving is allowed** (the website has no way out but Submit or the timer): it asks first, the clock keeps running, and the sitting can be resumed until time runs out, after which it is sent as it stands.
* **The screen is not kept awake** (no wake-lock plugin in `pubspec.yaml`); a long paper on a phone that sleeps simply resumes, since the clock does not depend on the screen.
* **Narrow phones**: at 320 dp (the screen tests' `a 320 dp phone with long names`) all three of the exam's buttons still show their words, a long value in the brief's table scales down to fit its column, the brief's Take test label scales down, and an awarding body's long name is cut with an ellipsis in its filter chip; nothing overflows.
* **The brief's heading has no dash** ("ASHRAE BEMP Set 1"), as the designs write it; the result's chip, the confirmations and the overview still use the website's "ASHRAE BEMP – Set 1". **The brief is a page, not a sheet over the landing**, so there is no 48 dp of the landing showing above it.
* **A sitting left unfinished is found again on that paper's brief** ("Resume - 18:00 left", or "Submit my saved answers" once the time has run out). There is no list of unfinished sittings in the app.
* **Landscape** works (the pane scrolls, the bars stay) but is not designed for; a rotation is proved not to lose anything.
