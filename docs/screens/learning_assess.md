# Assess Your Knowledge and Mock Exams

The Learning hub's first two doors. There is **no PNG** for them: the reference is the website's Learning Center (`AssessmentLanding`, `CourseAssessmentLevels`, `LevelChoiceStep`, `LevelPaperCard`, `MockTopicsLanding`, `MockTopicSets`, `SetCard`, `TopicCard`, `PaperCard`, `PaperUnlock`, `BundleOffer`, their `learnExams` copy) drawn in the app's own language: white cards with a 1 dp slate edge and a soft shadow, Work Sans titles, IBM Plex Sans body, text sized for a phone (13–16 dp, not measured from a 390 dp frame), 48 dp buttons, two columns for cards and one for anything with numbers in it.

The exam itself (the brief, the timer, the questions, the result) is **not here**: a **Start** button calls the exam builder's `openPaper(context, PaperRef(...))` (`lib/features/learning_exam/open_paper.dart`) and, when it returns, reads the member's sittings again.

## Screens

Four screens, built from two shared pieces (the door's landing, the group's page): the file layout is `lib/features/learning_assess/`.

- **`AssessmentScreen({required VoidCallback onBack, PaperOpener opener = openPaper, SiteLauncher launcher = openUrl, AssessRepository? repository})`** — Assess Your Knowledge. `TitleBar` "Assess Your Knowledge" with the bell (unread dot from `GET /notifications/unread-count`), the kit's search field ("Search certifications, e.g. contract"), the access facet (All · Free · Paid), the navy banner with the website's words (*Assess Your Knowledge. / Validate Your Expertise.* and its blurb, with Foundation · Professional · Advanced as three step chips), then ICR's own programmes in a two-column grid. A card: the cover (3:2, a "3 LEVELS" pill over it), the name (two lines), its levels in the order they are climbed ("Foundation · Professional · Advanced"), and a foot that says where **this member** stands: *Open* (everything open to them), *N of M open*, *In your plan* (a plan place would open all of it), *Locked*; then how they did: *N passed* / *All passed* (solid green) or *Best 40%* (amber). With the member's unlocks unknown it falls back to the catalogue's facts (*Free*, *Paid*, *Free + paid*). Under the grid, "Showing 2 of 4 certifications." when a search or the facet narrows it, and — only when a partner institute has published a programme — the website's line about where those are catalogued, with a "Partner certifications" link. A partner's programme is **not listed here** (the website does the same: it is catalogued under Professional Development).
- **`MockTestsScreen({same four})`** — Mock Exams. The same landing for the 24 subjects: banner (*Global Certification Mock Exams / Practice · Prepare · Perform*, steps Assess · Improve · Practice), "Search mock exams, e.g. energy, set 3", the facet, cards with the subject's cover, "2 SETS", "2 sets · 10 questions" and the same foot.
- **A programme's page** (pushed by a card, over the same `ShellPage` so the tab bar stays): the title bar names the programme; the cover with "Capital Planning Assessment" over its foot; **Knowledge areas covered** as chips (folded after four, *Show all 25*); the sale strip when a global discount is on; the plan card; one **card per rung** in the order they are climbed (Foundation, Professional, Advanced — see Deviations), a rung with no paper published greyed as *Coming soon*; the **bundle offer** (below) above the cards, so a member choosing a level sees it first.
- **A subject's page**: cover and name, "Exam sets / 3 sets, each with its own questions.", the plan card, one card per set ("SET 01", its name, its description or "Practice paper under exam conditions."); and the **bundle offer** for the exam's sets (`mock-bundles`), above the cards, drawn only while the exam is closed to the plan (`locked`: while a plan place could open it all the plan card is the offer).

### The bundle offer (programme and subject alike, owner's rule 27 Oct 2026)

One navy strip (`BundleOffer`, `widgets/bundle_offer.dart`), drawn only while the API quotes an offer for this member, with two offers that may share it: **in money** (`offered`: the priced levels or sets still closed, at the bundle discount; the struck separate price, the bundle price + GST, *Save ₹…*, *Offer valid till …* for a limited offer, **Unlock all N levels/sets on the website**) and **in ICR credits** (`creditsOffered`): *every* closed level or set, priced or not, at its own credits, **summed, less the bundle discount (10), never below the dearest one**: the API's `bundleCreditCost`. The strip says *BUNDLE · 5 CREDITS OFF*, *Open all 2 sets with ICR credits*, the struck *10 credits one at a time*, the bundle's *5 ICR credits*, *Save 5 ICR credits vs redeeming them one at a time*, and **Redeem 5 ICR credits** (filled gold when nothing here is priced, outlined beside the price: *Or redeem 13 ICR credits*). A sheet asks first (*5 ICR credits for “AEE CBCP”?* / *All 2 sets open for good. You will have 95 ICR credits left.*), then `POST …/redeem`: every card turns Unlocked at once, the balance drops, the courses opened are noted for the flash-card decks that follow them, and a snackbar says *2 sets opened with 5 ICR credits.* A balance that falls short turns the button off (*Needs 5; you have 3.* and *Invite colleagues to earn more*); the API's refusal is shown as it words it, and the offer is quoted again (a level opened meanwhile changes what is left). **The figures are the API's, shown as they come: the app never recomputes a bundle.** Every credit field is optional: production's API (main) has no credit bundle yet, so there the strip shows only the money offer, or nothing.

### A paper's card (level or set)

Header (a level: the round badge in its rung's colour, the rung's name, its tagline — *Build Your Basics*, *Prove Your Expertise*, *Go Deeper* — and a *CERTIFIED* or *N ATTEMPTS* chip; a set: *SET 01*, *Locked* or *N attempts*, the name, two lines of description), then:

1. **Questions · Duration · To pass** — the three numbers a candidate decides on.
2. **How they did**, once they have sat it: *Best score 80% (8/10)*, a bar with a tick at the pass mark, *Passed* / *Not passed yet*, *2 of 3 attempts used* (or *2 attempts*). Not yet sat, with a limit: *Up to 3 attempts*.
3. **What it takes**, by where the member stands: *Unlocked / Ready to start* (or *Opened with ICR credits*); *Included in your plan / Open this course with your plan to start its levels*; or, closed, the price — the list price struck through, the percent off, *₹374.25 + GST*, *You save ₹124.75*, *Pro Plus member price*, *or 4 ICR credits* — or the credits alone (*5 ICR credits / Worth ₹500*), or *Locked. Opens with ICR credits or a purchase.* while the quote has not arrived.
4. **The button**: **Start Assessment** / **Start test** (open), **Retake assessment** / **Retake test** (already sat), **No attempts left** (the paper's limit is used); **Open with my plan** (plan place free); closed: **Pay on the website** (priced) and **Redeem N ICR credits** (with *You have 100 credits*, or *Needs 5; you have 2* and the button off), **See plans** when it is neither.

### The plan card (above the cards)

The website's `LearningOpenCard`, in its words, drawn only for a member whose plans carry a number for this kind (not staff, not a plan that opens everything): *Open this course* with *Your plan includes 2 courses, and 1 is still free.* and **Open with my plan** (a confirmation first: *It takes one of the 2 courses your plan includes, and every level opens. None will be left.*); once opened, one line — *This course is open with your plan: every level is yours to sit.*; with the places used up or none included, *Unlock a level* with the plan line, *You have 100 ICR credits. 1 credit = ₹100…*, **See plans** / **See Pro Plus** (a mock exam shows none unless a plan on sale would open more of them) and, with no credits, *Invite colleagues to earn more*. Under it, a note in the owner's wording for each paper opened with credits: *You enrolled into this course “Capital Planning” – “Advanced” with ICR referral credits.*

## Wiring

The hub pushes either screen through the constructor (`AppShell.push(context, (c) => ShellPage(tab: AppTab.learning, child: AssessmentScreen(onBack: () => Navigator.of(c).pop())))`); nothing else is needed. **Each screen builds its own controller and repository** (a pushed route does not inherit the tab's providers): `AppConfig.demo ? DemoAssessRepository() : ApiAssessRepository(api: context.read<ApiClient>())`. `opener`, `launcher` and `repository` are seams for the tests; the app passes none. `PaperOpener` is `Future<void> Function(BuildContext, PaperRef)`: the real `openPaper` (whose extra optional `repository:` is for its own tests) is the default.

A card pushes its group's page through `AppShell.push` over a `ShellPage(tab: AppTab.learning)`, handing it the landing's `CatalogueController` (what the member holds is shared: a level opened on the page shows on the grid at once). `Start` awaits the opener and then reads the member's attempts again (`GroupController.afterSitting`), so the best score and the attempt count are fresh when the exam is closed.

## How "open to the member" is decided

The same rule as the API's (`LearningService.assertPaperOpen`) and the website's (`usePaperLocks`), in `MemberAccess.accessOf`: a paper in `paperIds` is **open**; otherwise its course (a level) or subject (a set) must be open: staff and plans with no number open all; else it is open when it is among the member's first `limit` plan places (`openedCourseIds` / `openedTopicIds`, oldest first). With a place still free (`opened.length < limit`) the paper is **planPlace** (the course asks to be opened, free); otherwise **closed** (credits or a payment, a paper at a time). A free-access paper is still closed to a member whose plan opens no course: the plan, not the price, is the gate (Bharat Buyer's plan opens none, so almost every level is closed to him and shows its credits). The live test checks this derivation against the API itself on 3 programmes and 2 subjects: our verdict equals the quote's `alreadyOpen` for every paper, and an open paper is served by `GET /learning/papers/:slug` while a closed one is refused `PAPER_LOCKED`.

## Deviations from the website

- **The level cards run Foundation, Professional, Advanced** (the ladder), not the website's Foundation, Advanced, Professional (owner's call, 1 Oct 2026, a choice of its three side-by-side columns). Stacked on a phone they read as the climb they are; `assessCardOrder` is one constant if the owner wants the website's order.
- **A partner institute's programme is left out** of Assess Your Knowledge, as on the website, and the page says where those are.
- **The search does not look in the knowledge areas** (it does look in the website's fields: title, code, summary, the papers' titles). A dozen programmes cover "contract", and a search for it should find Contract Management.
- The website's **photo tile** (picture, name, arrow, a share button) became a card with a foot that says where the member stands: the website reads those off its rail, a phone screen has nowhere else to show them.
- The sale strip, the discount ribbon and the bundle card keep the website's colours (green, orange-red, navy and gold) so a visitor from the site recognises them.
- Staff sign-ins (`allOpen`) see no lock, price or plan card anywhere, as on the website.

## The live data, and the one thing a live test cannot undo

The local API holds 31 assessment programmes, 24 mock subjects (the tests compare with whatever the API lists, not with these numbers), and the personas: Bharat Buyer (100 ICR credits; his plan opens no course and no mock exam unless `learning-study.ts --grant-bharat` is in, which gives him 12 plan places, Capital Planning's Foundation and Advanced opened with them, and — from the exam builder's seed — sittings on both) and Pooja Professional (180 credits, a number of sets opened, one empty sitting).

- `POST /learning/papers/:slug/redeem` opens a paper for good and the API has no route that takes it back. The live test that redeems (as **Pooja**, on the last set she does not hold and can afford) therefore **leaves that set open and the credits spent**: each full run uses one set and its credits (about 5 of her 180). When she holds every set she can afford it says so and skips instead of failing. Getting the tests right took ten runs, so as this was written Pooja holds **130** ICR credits and eleven sets opened with credits (the seed gave her 180 and one); the rest of the file leaves nothing behind.
- **The live file does not depend on Bharat's plan places** (the owner's grant of 12 may be in or out; it passes both ways, run 4 times: grant in twice, out once, in again). What needs an account with no places (a refused plan place, closed priced levels, the quotes, the sale and the bundle) runs as **Pooja**, who holds none; Bharat is used only where the expectation comes from the API's own answers (a quote's `alreadyOpen`, his unlock list, his attempt history). The API caches grants for a minute, so a run started right after the grant changes can still see the old state.
- The sale test changes the global discount **as ICR staff** (`PUT /learning/admin/assessment-pricing`) for a few seconds and puts it back in a `finally`; a run killed in the middle would leave 40 % / 50 % on, which the next run's first step reads and restores.
- The live widget tests run real calls outside the test's fake clock, pump rather than `pumpAndSettle` while a page loads (its progress bar animates for ever, and settling it would run the fake clock past the requests' own timeouts), and end by letting the screen's in-flight requests finish and the HTTP client's idle-connection timers fire.
- The portal allows 120 requests a minute per address and every live file shares it: the client waits out a 429, so a run alongside other builders' live files can take a minute longer.
