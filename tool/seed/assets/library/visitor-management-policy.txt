VISITOR MANAGEMENT POLICY
=========================

1. Purpose
Every visitor is registered, badged and escorted so the building knows who is inside at all times.

2. Before arrival
- The host pre-registers the visitor (name, company, vehicle, purpose, time).
- Contractors need a permit to work before they are admitted.

3. At the desk
- Photo ID is checked and logged; the badge shows the host, the floor and the expiry time.
- Devices that record are declared; restricted floors need a second approval.

4. Inside
- Visitors are escorted or badge-restricted to the floors they were approved for.
- Badges are returned at exit and the log is closed.

5. Review
The security lead reviews tailgating and unreturned-badge incidents monthly.
